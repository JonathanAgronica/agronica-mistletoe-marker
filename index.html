      Welcome

      G'day, and welcome to Mistletoe Marker!

      This free app from Agronica lets you walk your rows, tap LEFT or RIGHT when you pass mistletoe, and keep a GPS record you can export as KML and use to find those clumps again later. It works offline.

      Agronica is building a mistletoe modelling database to research the distribution and severity of mistletoe across districts. If you're happy to help, you can share your marks with us. It's completely optional.

      Sharers can request access to the combined district mistletoe data.

      What is shared (only if you opt in)
      • Mark locations, type (mistletoe or other issue), left/right side and time
      • Optional severity (Light / Moderate / Heavy) and variety
      • Optional thinned GPS track of where you walked
      • A random device ID. No name unless you choose to add one below

      Used for research into mistletoe distribution and severity. Turn sharing off anytime in Menu → Research & data sharing: uploads stop and anything still queued on your phone is deleted. Data already received stays in the research database - email jonathan@agronicaag.com to ask for removal.
